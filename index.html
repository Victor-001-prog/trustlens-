    How each check maps to Webacy's APIs

    wallet / contract
    Threat Risk + Contract Risk APIFlags sanctioned, drainer, or historically malicious addresses, and scores a contract's deployer history and vulnerabilities.
    token
    Token Analysis + Holder Analysis APISurfaces holder concentration, mint authority, and liquidity-lock status before a swap.
    link
    URL Risk APIChecks claim pages, mint sites, and airdrop links against known phishing and drainer patterns.

    Why a standalone checker

    Most Webacy integrations sit inside one dApp, for that dApp's own users. TrustLens is built the other way round: a free, public checker anyone entering Solana can paste a link or address into before they ever connect a wallet — the moment most scams actually land, on Telegram, X, and group chats, not inside an app.

    It's aimed squarely at the next wave of Solana users coming through communities like Superteam Nigeria, where onboarding volume is high and a single bad click costs a newcomer their first wallet. That's a distinct, complementary surface for Webacy's data — not a resubmission of an existing dApp's integration.
